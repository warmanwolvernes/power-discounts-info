Delete your Power Discounts account

Power Discounts is the Warman Wolverines team discount app, published on Google Play by Warman Wolverines Junior Hockey Organization Inc. You can delete your account and the data linked to it at any time, in either of these ways.

Option 1: Request deletion by email

Email admin@warmanwolverines.com with the subject Delete my Power Discounts account and include the mobile phone number that is on the team roster. You do not need the app installed to make this request.

Email a deletion request

We complete email requests within 30 days and confirm by reply.

Option 2: Delete it in the app

Open Power Discounts, go to Profile and tap Delete my account, then confirm. Your account and data are deleted immediately.

What gets deleted

  • Your roster profile: name, mobile phone number, role, jersey number and position.
  • Your redemption history.
  • Your sign-in account.
  • Saved offers stored on your phone (cleared when you delete in the app).

We do not keep any of this information after deletion unless the law requires us to. Deleting your account ends your access to the app.
