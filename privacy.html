Power Discounts Privacy Policy

Last updated October 30, 2026

Power Discounts is a private mobile app that gives members of the Warman Wolverines roster (players, staff and team managers) access to discounts from team sponsors. The app is operated by Warman Wolverines Junior Hockey Organization Inc. of Warman, Saskatchewan (the "Team"). This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Roster information entered by a team manager: your name, mobile phone number and role (player, staff or manager), and for players, jersey number and position.
  • Sign-in information: your mobile phone number, which is used to confirm you are on the roster and to text you a one-time sign-in code.
  • Redemption history: a record of the sponsor discounts you redeem, including the date and the offer.
  • Saved offers: offers you save to your wallet are stored only on your phone.
  • Sponsor logos that a team manager chooses to upload from their phone.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos (other than sponsor logos a manager uploads) or any payment information. The app contains no advertising, and we do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

How we use your information

  • To confirm you are an active member of the team roster.
  • To text you a one-time code so you can sign in without a password.
  • To show you the sponsor discounts available to you and let you redeem them.
  • To let team managers administer the roster and offers, and activate or deactivate a member's access.

How your information is stored and shared

Your information is stored in a database hosted by Supabase, our backend service provider, on servers located in the United States. Information is sent between the app and the database over encrypted connections. Inside the app, you can see only your own profile and redemption history; team managers can see the roster and redemption activity so they can run the program.

Sign-in codes are sent by text message through Twilio, our SMS provider, which receives your phone number for that purpose. These providers process information only to provide their services to us. We do not share your information with anyone else except where required by law.

Data retention

We keep your roster information and redemption history for as long as you are on the team roster. When you leave the team, a manager can deactivate or delete your record, and you can delete your account at any time as described below.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and data at any time:

  • In the app: open Profile and tap Delete my account. Your profile, redemption history and sign-in account are deleted immediately, and the saved offers on your phone are cleared.
  • By email or on the web: follow the steps on our account deletion page, or write to admin@warmanwolverines.com with the subject "Delete my Power Discounts account" and the mobile number on the roster.

Email requests are completed within 30 days. We do not keep any of your information after deletion unless the law requires us to.

Your choices and rights

You may ask us to review, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting your team manager or emailing admin@warmanwolverines.com. Because sign-in relies on your phone number matching the roster, deleting your information ends your access to the app.

Children's privacy

Power Discounts is intended for members of the team roster. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. A parent or guardian who believes we have collected such information should contact us so we can delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and the date at the top will be updated.
